## Five-post launch sequence

1. Missed-call teardown - overflow, after-hours demand, slow response, and dispatcher load, using sourced Invoca and ServiceTitan claims.
2. No-cooling demo - disclosure, danger screen, ZIP validation, provisional window, and dispatcher receipt.
3. Emergency restraint - water near electrical equipment, no troubleshooting, and illustrative handoff.
4. Dispatcher receipt - conversation-to-decision walkthrough with visible provenance.
5. Founding offer - `$1,000 setup, $750/month for 90 days, usage separate, first two customers`; operational launch guarantee only.

Every post links to `https://primearc.tech/fieldrelay/demo` except the offer post, which links to `https://primearc.tech/fieldrelay#audit`.

## Recording plan

The 90-second master and three short clips must be captured from a real staging call after provider credits, microphone capture, and the rotated key are verified. Do not ship silent screen recordings or synthetic “live” proof.

1. 0-10s: PrimeArcTech promise and visible AI/sandbox disclosure.
2. 10-20s: Select no-cooling scenario and click Talk to FieldRelay.
3. 20-55s: Interrupt once; supply fictional name, ZIP, and call details.
4. 55-72s: End call; show transcript and provenance.
5. 72-84s: Inspect provisional dispatcher receipt and safeguards.
6. 84-90s: `Put one missed call through the system.` plus demo URL.

Caption every spoken word. Never show provider keys, real caller data, balances, or an apparent real booking.
