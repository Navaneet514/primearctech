# PrimeArcTech Homepage Design Direction

## Design read

Founder-led AI systems studio for skeptical operators. Premium technical-editorial design, proof-first application funnel, direct language, and visible restraint.

- Redesign mode: targeted evolution
- Design variance: 8
- Motion intensity: 6
- Visual density: 4
- Theme: locked dark with mineral-white objects and panels
- Accent: signal orange `#D8643E`
- Shape rule: 14px controls, 16px media and panels, no decorative pills
- Type: Geist Sans and Geist Mono

## What stays

- Hero promise: `We build AI systems that complete real work.`
- PrimeArcTech wordmark, dark industrial palette, orange accent, hero image, and direct tone
- FieldRelay as a separate owned product and the strongest working proof
- Free AI Workflow Audit as the primary conversion action
- Existing routes, legal pages, security page, and FieldRelay ecosystem

## What changes

- Replace long studio/lab manifesto with a buyer-controlled situation selector.
- Move FieldRelay proof directly after the selector.
- Replace the prose-heavy fit and evidence sections with four inspectable proof artifacts.
- Offer one low-friction lead magnet: a free AI Workflow Audit reviewed directly.
- Turn the method into a visual five-day delivery rail.
- Remove the embedded homepage form. Keep qualification on `/build-brief`.
- Open FieldRelay product routes in a new tab from the parent studio.

### Danvaro

- Source: https://www.danvaro.com/

### Zenith

- Source: https://www.byzenith.co/
- Useful: strong hierarchy, founder-led positioning, services mapped to buyer state, early selected-work proof.
- Avoid: four service lanes widen choice and the visual language risks familiar AI-studio tropes.

### Zero Friktion

- Source: https://www.zerofriktion.com/
- Useful: fixed-clock framing, concrete artifacts, strong ownership language, straightforward process.
- Avoid: unsupported client proof, fake urgency, fake precision, excessive studio theater, and long copy.

## Conversion architecture

1. Hero: understand the promise in five seconds.
2. Situation selector: identify why PrimeArcTech is relevant.
3. Working proof: hear and inspect FieldRelay in a separate product experience.
4. Proof standard: see the artifacts that make AI systems operable.
5. Lead magnet: understand audit outputs and boundaries before applying.
6. Delivery rail: understand how PrimeArcTech takes a system from frame to monitored operation.
7. Closing conversion: request the free AI Workflow Audit on a dedicated page.

## Copy rules

- Concrete nouns and verbs.
- One paragraph per section, maximum 25 words by default.
- Zero em dashes or en dashes.
- No revenue promises, fake scarcity, client logos, testimonials, certifications, or invented outcomes.
- `Get free AI audit` is the one service CTA label everywhere.
- FieldRelay CTAs are a different product intent and open in a new tab.

## Visual rules

- The hero remains the only full-bleed photographic statement.
- Later images behave as evidence objects, not decorative backdrops.
- Use one asymmetric selector, one product specimen, one artifact wall, one offer sheet, and one delivery rail. Do not repeat a layout family.
- Motion communicates selection, hierarchy, and delivery progression only.
- All motion collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Mobile becomes strict single-column with 48px minimum controls and no horizontal overflow.
